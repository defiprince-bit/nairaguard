## 1. What NairaGuard is

NairaGuard is software: an AI agent on the Celo blockchain. It helps you keep your savings balanced between **cNGN** (a digital naira) and **USD₮** (a digital dollar), following limits **you** set.

You choose:
- **How much stays in naira** (your target naira share), or a preset: **Careful**, **Balanced** or **Active**
- A **daily max** the agent may trade
- An **"ask me above"** amount. Trades bigger than this wait for your approval.

When your mix drifts more than 5 points from your target, the agent may rebalance. It skips trades under $1 and explains every trade in plain English.

NairaGuard works in MiniPay, Valora, MetaMask and other Celo wallets. **NairaGuard is run by Douglas Godwin Michael. It is not run by, or affiliated with, MiniPay, Opera, Celo, Valora, MetaMask, or the issuers of cNGN or USD₮.**

## 4. What the agent can and cannot do

- The agent can **only** trade between cNGN and USD₮ inside your vault, and **only within the limits you set**. The vault contract enforces those limits.
- Trades above your "ask me above" amount need your approval. You have a **30-minute** approval window, and proposals **expire after 1 day** if you don't act.
- For safety, the agent checks a **Chainlink price floor** and **pauses** if price data is more than **1 hour old**.
- The agent can't withdraw your money to anyone else.
- Software can still make mistakes. Check your limits and your activity regularly.
